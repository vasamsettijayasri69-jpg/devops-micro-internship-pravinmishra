# Assignment 1. What is something you believe to be true that most people around you would disagree with?

### Rules

* No "safe" answers.
* Must be your real belief (not copied from internet).
* Minimum 50 words.

**Hint:** What do you believe about career, money, learning, discipline, relationships, health, success, life, tech industry, etc. that most people don't agree with?

## Answer

I believe that being consistent is more important than being naturally talented. Many people think that success mainly comes from having special skills or being very intelligent. I believe that someone who keeps learning, practises regularly, accepts mistakes, and continues even when progress is slow can achieve more than a talented person who does not put in consistent effort. For me, small improvements every day are more valuable than trying to become perfect immediately. I also believe that failure is not a sign that we should stop; it is feedback that helps us understand what to improve.

## Truth #1

### Truth

Consistent effort produces better results than trying to do everything perfectly from the beginning.

### Evidence from my life
When I started learning new technical concepts, I initially tried to understand everything at once. It made the learning process difficult and overwhelming.
When I started studying consistently in smaller steps and practising what I learned, I was able to understand concepts more clearly and make steady progress.

## Truth #2

### Truth

Learning by doing helps me understand technical concepts better than only reading about them.

### Evidence from my life

I noticed this while working with technical tools and concepts that were new to me. The first time I used them, I needed more time and made mistakes.
After repeating the process and practising it several times, I became more comfortable and could complete the same tasks with less difficulty.

## Truth #3

### Truth

Mistakes provide useful information about what I need to improve.

### Evidence from my life

Whenever I made mistakes while learning or completing a task, I reviewed what went wrong instead of simply giving up.
By identifying the reason for the mistake and trying again, I was able to avoid repeating the same problem and improve my understanding.

# Assignment 4. Have you ever cut corners (unethical / dishonest / shortcut behavior — not necessarily illegal)? If yes, how did it make you feel?

### Important

You don't need to write the full story.

Focus on the feeling:

* guilt
* fear
* shame
* stress
* regret
* numbness
* etc.

This is about self-awareness, not judgment.

### Answer Format

**Yes / No**

If Yes:Yes

**What emotion did you feel?** (minimum 50–100 words)

## Answer

I felt guilt and stress after taking a shortcut instead of doing something properly. At first, it felt easier because I saved time and finished the task quickly. However, afterward I kept thinking about whether I had made the right choice. I felt uncomfortable because I knew the result did not fully represent my genuine effort. This experience taught me that shortcuts may provide temporary relief, but they can create stress and reduce my confidence. Since then, I have tried to be more honest with my work and focus on doing things properly, even when it takes more time.
